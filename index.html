Vallon Privacy Policy

App: Vallon (com.vallon.app)
Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Vallon is a local personal firewall for Android. This policy explains what data Vallon processes on your device, what limited diagnostic or purchase-verification data may be transmitted off-device, and how Android permissions are used.

1. Local Firewall & Android VpnService

Vallon uses Android’s VpnService interface locally on your device to block network traffic for apps you do not allow. Apps you select as allowed bypass the local VPN interface and connect directly to the internet.

  • Vallon does not route your traffic through any remote VPN server.
  • Vallon does not inspect, log, store, or transmit your network packets, browsing history, DNS queries, or traffic contents.
  • All firewall rules, profiles, and schedules are stored locally on your device.

2. Installed Applications Inventory

Vallon requests access to the list of installed applications on your device (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES), including system and non-launchable network-capable apps, solely so you can choose which apps are allowed to connect and so Vallon can apply per-UID firewall rules locally.

Your installed-app list and app selections never leave your device.

3. Crash Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

Release builds of Vallon include Google Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and stability issues. When crash reporting is active, Crashlytics may transmit:

  • Crash stack traces and app state at the time of a crash
  • Device metadata (such as OS version and device model)
  • Firebase installation and session identifiers

Vallon does not attach your selected app rules, installed-app list, or network packet contents to crash reports. For more information on how Google processes Firebase Crashlytics data, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. Subscriptions & Purchase Verification (Vallon Plus)

If Vallon Plus subscriptions are enabled and you choose to subscribe, payments are processed by Google Play. Vallon does not collect or store your payment card details.

To verify active subscription entitlement, Vallon transmits your Google Play purchaseToken over HTTPS to the configured verification service. The token is stored locally in private no-backup app storage on your device so your entitlement can be re-verified.

5. No Accounts, Ads, or Usage Analytics

  • Vallon does not require user accounts or login credentials.
  • Vallon does not include advertising SDKs or ad-ID tracking.
  • Vallon does not include behavioral usage analytics.

6. Permissions Used

  • BIND_VPN_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SYSTEM_EXEMPTED: Runs the local on-device firewall service and displays its ongoing status notification.
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Displays installed network-capable apps locally so you can manage firewall rules.
  • INTERNET: Used by Firebase Crashlytics crash reporting and Google Play purchase verification (not used for remote VPN tunneling).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional permission to show the ongoing firewall notification.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Optional user-granted permission used only for timed Plus routines (schedules, focus sessions, and temporary allowances).

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Firewall rules, routines, and locally cached receipt tokens remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall Vallon. Because Vallon does not create user accounts, there is no account profile to delete on our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if app features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
